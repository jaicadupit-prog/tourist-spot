Activities & Adventures

Unforgettable Island Experiences

From diving into deep ocean trenches to flying over volcanic peaks, the Philippines offers adrenaline and serenity alike.

Island Hopping

Charter a traditional wooden *banca* boat and sail between secluded coves, hidden lagoons, and sandbars.

World-Class Diving

Explore Tubbataha Reefs, swim alongside gentle Whale Sharks in Apo Island, or dive WWII shipwrecks in Coron.

Volcano & Mountain Treks

Hike the crater lake of Mt. Pinatubo, admire the symmetrical cone of Mt. Mayon, or scale Mt. Apo.

Vibrant Festivals

Dance to traditional beats at Sinulog, Ati-Atihan, or MassKara—celebrating life with color and music.

Rich Heritage & Flavors

Taste & Feel the Filipino Warmth

Filipino food is a soulful fusion of Malay origins, Spanish heritage, American influences, and Chinese flavors. Combined with legendary Filipino hospitality, every meal feels like a celebration.

🍲

Chicken/Pork Adobo

🐖

🍨

Halo-Halo

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

The dry season runs from November to April, making it the ideal time to travel. Peak summer months are March to May, perfect for beach activities and island hopping.
Tourists from over 150 countries (including USA, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, and ASEAN nations) can enter visa-free for up to 30 days, provided they hold a valid return ticket.
Filipino (Tagalog) is the national language, but English is widely spoken across all islands as an official language. Navigating, dining, and chatting with locals in English is effortless!
Domestic airlines (Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, AirAsia) connect main cities. Fast ferries and local motorized outrigger boats (*outriggers/bancas*) connect neighboring islands.

Power Socket: Type A, B, and C (220V, 60Hz). Bring a universal adapter.
SIM & Data: Local eSIMs or SIM cards (Globe / Smart) available at international airports upon arrival.
